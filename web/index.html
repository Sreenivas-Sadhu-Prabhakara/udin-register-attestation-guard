UDIN register and generation deadline for every document you sign

One register of every certificate, audit report and attestation: who signed it, for which client and financial year, against which figures, and whether its UDIN exists yet. The deadline is counted from the date of signing.

ICAI UDIN mandate Stays on this machine
This tool does not generate UDINs, and cannot. Only ICAI's portal issues a UDIN, and only the signing member can log in and generate one. There is no connection to the portal here and no automation of it: this register records the number you generated and counts the days. A number that passes its shape check is not thereby a real UDIN — only verification on the portal can tell you that.
